Digital fitness platform combining training programs, ecommerce, and an affiliate content blog — built and operated end-to-end as founder and technical lead.

Overview

DG Athlete offered structured home fitness training programs alongside an ecommerce storefront and an affiliate/SEO-driven blog. Training programs were delivered as authored PDF guides paired with hands-on personal training, rather than an in-app feature — the platform's role was content delivery, commerce, and audience growth.

Tech Stack

  • WordPress — CMS for the site and affiliate blog
  • WooCommerce — ecommerce storefront and payment processing
  • WP Engine — managed hosting
  • Custom CSS/JS — hand-built frontend on top of the WordPress theme layer
